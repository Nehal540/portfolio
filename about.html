A little about me.

Hi, I’m Nehal Thakkar, an interaction designer curious about creating screens and experiences that make technology feel intuitive, playful, and human.

Outside work mode, you’ll usually find me lost in a book, exploring a game, or heading out on a trek. Books are my escape into some other world, while games have kept me curious since ninth grade about worlds I can actually take part in. Sometimes, I like to swap the screen for a trail and let curiosity pick the route in ways of exploring that shape how I see and design.
